# Privacy Policy – MyApplication

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

MyApplication ("the app", package `com.alfred.myapplication`) is published by Alfred Gerogiannis ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

## 1. Data we collect

| Data | Why | Shared with |
|---|---|---|
| Email address, display name, account ID | To create and secure your account (sign-in, password reset) | Google Firebase Authentication |
| In-app points, settings and profile data | To provide app features and sync your profile | Stored on your device and in Firebase |
| Device information, app interactions, crash and diagnostics data | To keep the app working and improve it | Google Firebase (Analytics, Cloud Messaging) |
| Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (derived from IP), device and ad-interaction data | To show ads, measure them, and prevent ad fraud | Google AdMob and its mediation partner Unity Ads |
| Push notification token | To send notifications you can turn off at any time | Firebase Cloud Messaging |

We do not sell your personal data. We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

## 2. Advertising and consent

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, with Unity Ads as a mediation partner. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states) the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Third-party policies:
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google ads partners: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
- Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 3. Rewarded ads

Some features let you choose to watch an ad in exchange for in-app points. Watching is always optional. Points have no cash value.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data at any time:
- **In the app:** Profile → Delete account.
- **Without the app:** email **alfredgerogiannis8@gmail.com** from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account and associated data within 30 days.

Some data may be kept longer where the law requires it, or in anonymised form by our service providers under their own policies.

## 5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Account security is handled by Google Firebase.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
